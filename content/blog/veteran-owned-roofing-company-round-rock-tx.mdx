## What "SBA Veteran-Owned Certified" Actually Means

Veteran-owned business certification in the United States is administered directly by the Small Business Administration through its VetCert program. To get certified, a business has to submit documentation proving:

- The business is at least 51% owned by one or more veterans
- Those veterans control day-to-day operations and long-term decision-making
- Military service is verified against official service records

A federal reviewer checks all of it before certification is issued. That's a meaningfully different bar than a business owner simply deciding to call their company "veteran-owned," which — legally — almost anyone can do without ever proving it.

Ripple Roofing's certification is specifically a **Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB)** designation, not Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned (SDVOSB), which is a separate category with its own eligibility requirements. We're not going to blur that line just because SDVOSB sounds more impressive.
