## How Central Texas Weather Affects Shingle Roofs

Hutto sits right in the path of the storms that roll through Central Texas each spring and fall, and the heat here is tough on roofing materials year-round.

**Hail** is the biggest threat to asphalt shingles in our area. Even moderate hail can bruise or crack shingles, knocking loose the granules that protect them from sun damage. That damage isn't always obvious from the ground, which is why a post-storm inspection matters.

**High winds** can lift shingle edges and tear off sections entirely, especially on roofs where the sealant strips have already weakened with age.

**Heat and UV exposure** are the slow, steady wear-and-tear factors. Texas summers push shingles through repeated expansion and contraction, which speeds up aging and can cause curling, cracking, or granule loss over time.

If you're curious how long a shingle roof typically holds up under these conditions, our guide on [how long a roof lasts in Texas](https://www.rippleroofs.com/blog/how-long-does-a-roof-last-texas) breaks it down in plain terms.

## Signs Your Hutto Roof Needs Repair vs. Replacement

Not every roof problem means you need a full replacement. Here's how to tell the difference:

**Signs a repair may be enough:**
- A handful of missing or cracked shingles in one area
- A single active leak with clear, localized cause
- Minor flashing issues around a chimney or vent
- Your roof is under 15 years old with no widespread wear

**Signs you likely need a replacement:**
- Shingles are curling, cracking, or losing granules across large sections of the roof
- Multiple leaks in different areas
- Your roof is nearing or past its expected lifespan
- Storm damage is widespread rather than isolated
- You've had repeat repairs on the same roof within a couple of years

Our detailed comparison in [Roof Replacement vs. Repair: A Homeowner's Guide](https://www.rippleroofs.com/blog/roof-replacement-vs-repair-guide) walks through this decision in more depth, and our [DIY roof inspection checklist](https://www.rippleroofs.com/resources/roof-inspection-checklist) can help you do a preliminary check yourself before calling us out.
