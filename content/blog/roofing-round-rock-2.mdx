## Round Rock Weather & What It Means for Your Roof

Round Rock roofs face three main stressors year-round:

### Hail

Central Texas sees regular hail events, and Round Rock has been in the path of several damaging storms in recent years. Even hail that doesn't puncture shingles can bruise the mat underneath, shortening the roof's life. If a storm has recently passed through, our [Round Rock hail damage guide](https://www.rippleroofs.com/blog/round-rock-hail-damage-guide) and [storm damage assessment breakdown](https://www.rippleroofs.com/blog/round-rock-storm-damage-assessment) explain what to look for.

### Heat

Summer roof surface temperatures in Round Rock can climb well above typical air temperature, with some estimates exceeding 150°F on exposed shingles. Poor attic ventilation compounds the problem, aging shingles faster and driving up cooling costs. Proper ventilation is worth discussing during any inspection or replacement.

### Wind

Strong spring storm systems bring straight-line winds capable of lifting shingle edges or loosening flashing, especially on older roofs. Regular inspections catch this before it turns into a leak.
