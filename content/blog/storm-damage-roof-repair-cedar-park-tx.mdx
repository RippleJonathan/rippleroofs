# Storm Damage Roof Repair in Cedar Park, TX

Cedar Park sits right in the path of Central Texas's toughest spring and summer storm systems, and it doesn't take a headline-grabbing hailstorm to leave your roof compromised. If you just weathered high winds, hail, or falling debris, here's what to look for and how our local crew responds.

## What Cedar Park Storm Damage Actually Looks Like

Most storm damage in our area falls into three categories: wind, hail, and falling limbs. Wind gusts common to Cedar Park can lift shingle edges or peel back entire sections, especially on roofs that are already a few years past their prime. Hail — even small hail moving fast — bruises asphalt shingles and dents metal panels, creating weak spots that aren't always visible from the ground. Fallen limbs from the mature oaks common throughout Cedar Park neighborhoods can puncture decking or crack tiles outright.

The reason quick attention matters isn't drama — it's physics. A small hail bruise or lifted shingle tab lets water find its way under your roofing system. Once moisture reaches the decking, you're looking at rot, mold, and interior damage that costs far more to fix than the original roof issue. Learn more about how we assess this kind of damage in our [storm damage restoration guide](https://www.rippleroofs.com/blog/storm-damage-restoration-guide).

## Signs Your Roof Took Damage After a Central Texas Storm

After any significant wind or hail event, walk your property (safely, from the ground) and check for:

- **Granules in gutters or downspouts** — a sign asphalt shingles have been bruised by hail
- **Shingles that look curled, cracked, or missing** — especially on the roof faces that took the brunt of the wind
- **Dents or dings on gutters, vents, or AC units** — if these metal surfaces show impact marks, your roof likely did too
- **Soft or discolored spots on ceilings** — a delayed sign of a leak that started during the storm
- **Debris or broken limbs on the roofline** — even if nothing looks obviously wrong, hidden puncture damage is possible

If you notice any of these, it's worth a professional look before the next rain finds the weak spot for you. Our [roof inspection checklist](https://www.rippleroofs.com/resources/roof-inspection-checklist) is a good starting point if you want to do a preliminary check yourself.

## Shingle vs. Metal Roof Storm Repairs — What to Expect

**Shingle roofing** is the most common storm-repair job we see in Cedar Park. Hail bruising and wind-lifted tabs are usually straightforward to address by replacing the affected shingles and matching color and style as closely as possible. If damage is widespread across multiple roof planes, a full roof replacement often makes more sense than patchwork repairs — we'll walk you through that reasoning honestly.

**Metal roofing**, including standing seam systems, holds up well against hail and wind but isn't invincible. Repairs typically involve addressing seam separation, fastener loosening, or panel dents rather than widespread material failure. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my Cedar Park roof has storm damage?

Look for granules collecting in gutters, curled or missing shingles, dents on gutters or vents, soft spots on ceilings, and debris on the roofline. If you spot any of these signs, it's worth having a professional take a closer look.

### Does hail damage always mean I need a full roof replacement?

Not necessarily. Many hail-related issues, like bruised shingles or a few dented metal panels, can be repaired. Widespread damage across multiple roof planes is when replacement often makes more sense — we'll walk you through the reasoning for your specific roof.

### Do metal roofs hold up better than shingles in Central Texas storms?

Metal roofing, including standing seam systems, generally resists hail and wind better than asphalt shingles, but it can still develop seam or fastener issues after a severe storm. Both materials can be repaired when damage is caught early.
